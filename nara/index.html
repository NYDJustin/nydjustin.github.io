What Nara can do

Most chatbots forget you the moment the conversation ends. Nara is built the other way around — memory first, and everything else grows from there.

🧠 She remembers you

Long-term memory is Nara's heart: she keeps short notes about your name, interests, and plans — in two strictly separated segments, public and private — and recalls what matters when it matters.

🔎 Live web search

Ask about today's news, prices, or scores. Nara searches the web across several providers and answers with sources, instead of guessing from stale training data.

🎨 Images & sound effects

She draws pictures on request and generates sound effects — rain, thunder, a busy café — delivered straight into your chat.

🔊 Voice in, voice out

Send her a voice note and she understands it; ask for a spoken reply and she talks back. Speech runs on a multi-provider cascade with a local fallback.

⏰ Reminders, watches & tasks

One-off reminders, recurring schedules, topic watches that DM you when something new appears, and background research tasks she completes while you do other things.

🎲 Games & fun

Trivia, word-chain, and polls for your server or your DMs — plus polls with real buttons and live results.

✦ ✦ ✦

Her memory, your rules

Nara keeps two kinds of notes about you, and the wall between them is hard:

🌐 Public memory

Things learned in servers and public chats. These notes follow you across servers and DMs, so she recognizes you wherever you meet.

🔒 Private memory

Things learned in your DMs. These never leave your DM — they are never shown or used in any server or group chat. Ever.

You stay in charge: /memory shows your notes, /fix edits one, /forget deletes one, and /reset erases everything. The full details live in the Privacy Policy.
